**Download** `OpenVoiceType-<version>.dmg` below, open it and drag OpenVoiceType to Applications. The first time, macOS
says it can't verify the app: click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. The
[README](https://github.com/mahfuzur/openvoicetype#install) has the details.

**Coming from Voice to Text 0.1.x?** The app has a new name, OpenVoiceType, and to macOS that makes it a new app:
- Grant **Microphone** and **Accessibility** once more. The app asks for both when it starts.
- Your settings (hotkey, models, modes, cleanup choices) are copied over automatically. Your dictionary, config and logs
  stay where they were.
- It offers to move the old **Voice to Text** app to the Trash. If you had it open at login, turn that on again in
  **Settings → General**.

How OpenVoiceType uses your Claude Code, and what Anthropic's terms say about it:
[docs/TERMS.md](https://github.com/mahfuzur/openvoicetype/blob/master/docs/TERMS.md).
